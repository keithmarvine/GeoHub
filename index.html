01 Our Approach

No boundaries.
No borders.
Just precision.

GeoHuB | The Spatial Network is a globally operating geospatial engineering firm. We work entirely remotely — delivering precision GIS analysis, spatial intelligence, and web mapping solutions to clients anywhere in the world, without ever needing to be on-site.

From satellite imagery over Sub-Saharan Africa to land suitability studies in Southeast Asia, we harness cloud-based tools and remote sensing technology to serve governments, NGOs, and private enterprises across every continent.

Remote Data Acquisition

We source and process satellite imagery, aerial data, and existing spatial datasets — no field visit required. Clients supply context; we deliver analysis.

Analyse & Model

Advanced cloud-based spatial analysis, remote sensing interpretation, and multi-criteria modelling — run entirely online and delivered as reports, maps, or datasets.

Build & Deploy

Web GIS platforms, interactive dashboards, and spatial APIs — hosted in the cloud and accessible to your team from anywhere in the world.
