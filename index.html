08 How We Work

Remote delivery,
without the guesswork.

Working with a team you have never met should not feel like a risk. Here is exactly how a GeoHuB project runs, from first message to final files.

  1. 01

    You send the brief

    A short description of the problem, the area of interest, and the format you need. If you are unsure what to ask for, say so — scoping the work properly is our job, not yours.

  2. 02

    We scope and quote — free

    Within 24 hours you receive a written approach: the method, the data sources, the deliverables, the timeline, and a fixed price. No obligation and no charge for this stage.

  3. 03

    We produce the work

    Analysis runs on satellite, survey and open data using ArcGIS, QGIS, PostGIS and Google Earth Engine. You get progress updates at agreed checkpoints, not silence until the deadline.

  4. 04

    You review, we revise

    Deliverables arrive in the formats your team already uses — DWG, SHP, GeoTIFF, GeoJSON or print-ready PDF. Revisions are part of the scope, not an upsell.
